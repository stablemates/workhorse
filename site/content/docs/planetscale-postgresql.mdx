---
title: Can I use Workhorse with PlanetScale PostgreSQL?
description: Connect Workhorse to PlanetScale PostgreSQL through the standard PostgreSQL drivers, with the correct connections and permissions.
---

**Yes, PlanetScale PostgreSQL is expected to work with Workhorse.** PlanetScale supplies a
PostgreSQL service, and Workhorse uses standard PostgreSQL drivers and SQL. Use the existing SDK for
your language. You do not need a PlanetScale adapter.

This statement is expected compatibility. It does not claim a Workhorse test against PlanetScale. It
applies to the regular PostgreSQL service of PlanetScale, not to MySQL/Vitess or Neki.

To get credentials for the writable primary, follow the
[PostgreSQL connection instructions](https://planetscale.com/docs/postgres/connecting) of
PlanetScale.

## Install the schema through a direct primary connection

Use a direct primary connection on port `5432` for the schema setup. PlanetScale recommends direct
connections for DDL and schema changes. Workhorse manages its own schema, separately from the
migrations of your application.

## Give the worker a direct primary connection pool

Run a dedicated worker process with a direct primary connection pool. A direct connection keeps the
session that the `LISTEN` wake hints need.

## Connect the application directly or through PgBouncer

The application can use direct connections. It can also use the transaction-mode PgBouncer of
PlanetScale on port `6432`.

- If a business write and its enqueue must commit together, give Workhorse the same transaction
  connection.
- Through transaction pooling, do not depend on `LISTEN` or on session-level state.

## Verify TLS and keep the credentials secret

PlanetScale requires TLS. For libpq clients, it documents `sslmode=verify-full`.

1. Verify the server certificate and the hostname.
2. Use an equivalent certificate check in your driver. Do not turn off the check.
3. Keep the credentials in your secret store.

## Use roles with the correct permissions

Use an authorized schema owner to install and migrate the schema. Read and write grants on the data
do not permit schema creation or DDL. Workers also do maintenance, so make sure that their role has
the necessary permissions.

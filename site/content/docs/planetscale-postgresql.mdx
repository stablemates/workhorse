---
title: Can I use Workhorse with PlanetScale PostgreSQL?
description: Expected compatibility through standard PostgreSQL drivers, with connection and permission considerations.
---

**Yes, PlanetScale PostgreSQL should work with Workhorse.**
PlanetScale provides a PostgreSQL service, and Workhorse uses standard PostgreSQL drivers and SQL.
Use the existing SDK for your language; no PlanetScale adapter is needed.
This is expected compatibility, not a claim that Workhorse has been tested against PlanetScale.
It applies to PlanetScale's regular PostgreSQL service, not MySQL/Vitess or Neki.

## Connection considerations

Follow PlanetScale's [PostgreSQL connection instructions](https://planetscale.com/docs/postgres/connecting) to obtain credentials for the writable primary.

- **Schema setup:** use a direct primary connection on port `5432`.
  PlanetScale recommends direct connections for DDL and schema changes.
  Workhorse manages its own schema separately from your application migrations.
- **Worker:** run a dedicated worker process with a direct primary connection pool.
  Direct connections preserve the session needed for `LISTEN` wake hints.
- **Application:** direct connections or PlanetScale's transaction-mode PgBouncer on port `6432` can serve application transactions.
  When a business write and enqueue must commit together, pass the same transaction connection to Workhorse.
  Do not depend on `LISTEN` or session-level state through transaction pooling.
- **TLS and credentials:** verify the server certificate and hostname, and keep credentials in your secret store.
  PlanetScale requires TLS and documents `sslmode=verify-full` for libpq clients.
  Use equivalent certificate verification in your driver; do not disable it.
- **Permissions:** use an authorized schema owner for installation and migrations.
  Data-read and data-write grants alone do not permit schema creation or DDL.
  Workers also perform maintenance, so ensure their role has the required permissions.

PlanetScale documents these details in its [PgBouncer guide](https://planetscale.com/docs/postgres/connecting/pgbouncer),
[connection quickstart](https://planetscale.com/docs/postgres/connecting/quickstart) and
[role documentation](https://planetscale.com/docs/postgres/connecting/roles).
Those sources were reviewed on **2026-10-02**; the catalog date records that documentation review, not a provider test.

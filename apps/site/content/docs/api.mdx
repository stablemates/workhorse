guide links for behavior and the published declarations for exact types.

## Database and framework adapters

Core exports `Queryable`, `createWorkhorseAdapter`, `WorkhorseAdapter`, and
`WorkhorseAdapterOptions`.

`@workhorse/drizzle` exports `createDrizzleAdapter`, `drizzleQueryable`, `DrizzleExecutor`,
`DrizzleAdapterOptions`, and `DrizzleQueryError`.

`@workhorse/hono` exports `HonoWorkhorse`, `HonoWorkhorseContext`, `HonoWorkhorseEnv`,
`HonoWorkerDefinition`, `HonoWorkhorseOptions`, `serveWithWorkhorse`,
`ServeWithWorkhorseOptions`, `HonoWorkhorseServer`, `mountWorkhorseDashboard`, and
`MountWorkhorseDashboardOptions`.

`@workhorse/dashboard` exports `Dashboard`, `DashboardProps`, `WorkhorseThemeProvider`,
`DashboardColorScheme`, and `useWorkhorseTheme`.

Notification exports are `DashboardNotifications`, `DashboardNotification`,
`dashboardNotificationPosition`, `notifyDashboard`, `notifyFailure`, and
`clearDashboardNotifications`.

Client types are `DashboardAuditInput`, `DashboardClient`, `DashboardDemoJobKind`,
`DashboardDemoScenario`, and `DashboardDemoTools`.

Location exports are `parseTaskLocation`, `taskDetailNavigation`, `taskListingKey`,
`taskLocationHref`, `taskPageSizes`, `TaskActivityGroup`, `TaskActivityPeriod`,
`TaskDetailNavigation`, `TaskLocationState`, and `TaskPageSize`.

`@workhorse/dashboard/client` exports `createDashboardClient`.

`@workhorse/dashboard/server` exports `createDashboardHost`, `DashboardHost`,
`DashboardHostOptions`, `dashboardNodeMiddleware`, and `DashboardNodeMiddleware`.

Its router and readers are `dashboardRouter`, `DashboardRouter`, `DashboardRpcContext`,
`readDashboardEvents`, `readDashboardSnapshot`, and `readDashboardWorkers`.

Its reader type is `DashboardEventsQuery`. Its database exports are `dashboardDatabase`,
`DashboardDatabase`, `DashboardSql`, and `sql`.

Host helpers are `dashboardAssetsDirectory`, `normalizeDashboardPath`, `renderDashboardHtml`,
`DashboardRuntimeConfig`, `RenderDashboardHtmlOptions`, `DASHBOARD_BROWSER_MODULES_PLACEHOLDER`, and
`DASHBOARD_RUNTIME_CONFIG_PLACEHOLDER`.

Its control types are `DashboardAuditContext`, `DashboardCancelTaskResult`,
`DashboardDurabilityProjector`, `DashboardOperator`, `DashboardQueueController`,
`DashboardScheduleController`, `DashboardTaskController`, and `DashboardWorkerController`.

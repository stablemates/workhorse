---
title: How do I limit how quickly work starts?
description: Synchronize PostgreSQL-owned queue and keyed token buckets across every worker.
---

Concurrency limits active work. `Queue.syncRateLimitPolicies` limits starts over time, even when
tasks finish immediately.

## Synchronize desired policy

```ts
await queue.syncRateLimitPolicies("workers", [
  {
    queue: "provider-api",
    rate: { limit: 60, intervalMs: 60_000, burst: 10 },
    perKey: { limit: 5, intervalMs: 60_000, burst: 2 },
  },
]);
```

The queue bucket admits sixty starts per minute and retains ten tokens after idle time. Every
non-null `concurrencyKey` also receives an independent five-per-minute bucket with a burst of two.

PostgreSQL supplies refill time and consumes tokens in the claim transaction. Application clock
skew cannot create capacity, and competing worker processes cannot spend the same token.

## Observe throttling

`Queue.rateLimitStatuses()` reports available queue tokens, bounded throttled-ready depth, throttled
key count, and the earliest sampled eligibility time. `Queue.health()` includes the same summary,
and OpenTelemetry exports queue-level configured rate, tokens, throttle depth, and eligibility delay.

The Queues page keeps concurrency and start rate in separate columns. This makes an active-work cap
visually distinct from a ready task waiting for its next token.
